About After Sixty Money

After Sixty Money explains Social Security, Medicare and 401(k) math in plain numbers for Americans near retirement. Official sources, no advice.

Who makes this

Vision Media publishes After Sixty Money on YouTube. We research and write every script ourselves. The voice you hear is text-to-speech, and each video description says so.

How we work

Each figure in a video comes from a source we name, government first: SSA, CMS, Medicare.gov, the IRS. You'll find those sources in the video description and at the bottom of each article. Pictures are public domain, openly licensed or made by us, and the credits page lists them video by video.
